# Draft: cat care tasks from three more sources

DRAFT FOR REVIEW. Not part of the knowledge base yet, and not for retrieval.
General guidance, not veterinary advice. Quotes are exact (text extracted from the PDFs, so line breaks
and some characters may differ). The Alan Edwards book has no reliable page numbers in the extraction;
chapter names are given instead. Check every quote against the PDF before citing it.

Priority column: what the proposed rule (HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW from the source's wording) would give.
It is my interpretation, for you to accept or change.

## D. Issues for you to decide

1. The sources disagree about senior age. AAFP-AAHA: Mature 7-10 y, Senior 11-14 y, Geriatric 15+ y.
Cornell: senior from 10 y, with vet visits twice a year. The system needs one staging source per
species. Proposal: use AAFP-AAHA for stages, and note that Cornell's "10 y" falls in its Mature stage.
2. Flea, worm and heartworm treatments are products. Our guardrail refuses medication and dosage.
Proposed: the task reads "Parasite control: ask your vet", with the cited schedule, and never names a product or amount.
3. Worming differs. AAFP-AAHA: kitten every 2 weeks from 3-9 weeks, then monthly to 6 months, and fecal
exams 1-2 times a year for adults. Edwards: "every 6 months: worm treatment". Different life stages,
different eras. Proposal: use the newer veterinary guideline, and do not combine numbers across sources.
4. The Edwards book is a 1999/2009 commercial book. Vaccine and parasite advice may be dated. Proposal: use
it only for routine owner tasks (feeding, water, litter, grooming) and not for medical schedules.
5. The e-book is the only ranking source and has weak authority. Options: (a) do not use it, and rank from
the veterinary sources' own wording; (b) use it for the ordering only, with its weakness stated. Your call.
6. Daily owner tasks now have numbers: litter at least once a day (Cornell, Edwards), water at least once
a day (Edwards), meals once or twice a day for adults (Edwards). Durations still have no source. Under
our rules these tasks would be declined for duration unless you decide duration is optional. That decision
is open.
