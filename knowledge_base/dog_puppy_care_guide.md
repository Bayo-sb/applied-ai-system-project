# Dog care: Puppy Care Guide (Greencross Vets and Petbarn)

General guidance, not veterinary advice.

Species: dog
Stages: all

## Source

- Title: "Puppy Care Guide"
- Author: Greencross Vets and Petbarn (Australia), 60-page guide
- Local copy: `assets/498223126-Puppy-Care-Guide.pdf` (not committed to the repository)
- A retailer's brochure for Australian owners, with product advertising. Authority is weaker than a veterinary guideline, and some advice is specific to Australia. Quotes are short exact phrases. The rest is written in my own words.

## Puppy and adult stages

The guide treats a dog as a puppy until it is about a year old. Its timeline puts the switch at "12 to 18 months", when dogs are "classified as adults" and move to adult dog food. It says "Small and medium breeds typically become adults at 12 months, while it can take up to 24 months for giant breeds to mature". This system has no size or breed field, so it uses 12 months for every dog and does not model giant breeds.

## Feeding puppies

Stages: puppy

Young puppies eat several small meals a day. After about four months the guide says it is still fine to feed several small meals, and that this "can slowly decrease to twice daily": "3-4 small meals per day". Puppy food is meant to "help them grow strong".

```claim
id: dog-feed-puppy
task: Feed puppy meals
cadence: daily
cadence_text: 3-4 small meals a day, slowly decreasing to twice a day
cadence_quote: 3-4 small meals per day
importance: essential
importance_quote: help them grow strong
medication: no
```

## Daily walks for puppies

Stages: puppy

"Exercise is a must", the guide says, but a puppy "shouldn't be taken outside until two weeks after their final core vaccination", which is typically at 14-16 weeks. Until then, check with the veterinarian. Once walking, "Short, gentle walks taken daily" suit a growing puppy better than one big walk every couple of days.

```claim
id: dog-walk-puppy
task: Short daily walk (check with your veterinarian first)
cadence: daily
cadence_text: short walks, daily
cadence_quote: Short, gentle walks taken daily
importance: essential
importance_quote: Exercise is a must
medication: no
```

## Puppy vaccination

Stages: puppy

Vaccination is medication, so this system always sends it to a human with the note to ask the veterinarian. The guide gives a puppy schedule: "First vaccination between 6-8 weeks old", a booster between 10-12 weeks and a final puppy vaccination between 14-16 weeks. It explains why: "It is necessary to build your dog's immunity when they are young". It states no product and no dose.

```claim

task: Puppy vaccinations (ask your veterinarian)
cadence: other

cadence_quote: First vaccination between 6-8 weeks old
```
