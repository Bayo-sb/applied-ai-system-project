## Grooming needs differ for every dog

Grooming needs depend on breed, coat type, age and activity level. Regular grooming matters for a dog's health and wellbeing.

## Make a grooming schedule by observing the dog

Veterinarians often suggest building a grooming schedule. Start by watching the dog and noting how often it needs a bath, a trim and a brush. Useful signs to watch for:

- when the dog starts to itch after its last bath
- how oily or dull the coat becomes
- how quickly the nails grow

These things change as the dog gets older, so the schedule should be adjusted over time.

## Bathing

- Do not bathe too often. Frequent baths can dry out the skin and coat and cause problems.
- Use a shampoo made for dogs. Do not use human products.
- A veterinarian can help choose a shampoo that suits the dog's coat.
- Avoid medicated shampoos unless a veterinarian recommends one.
- If the dog may have a skin condition, get an expert opinion.

## Other grooming needs

Besides bathing, a dog's grooming includes:

- dental care
- nail trimming
- haircuts and brushing
- ear and eye care

## What this source does not cover

This source supports general grooming considerations. It does not specify numerical task frequencies, task durations, or priority levels. These values must not be inferred from this document.
