General guidance, not veterinary advice.

Species: cat
Stages: all

## Feeding adult cats

Stages: adult, mature, senior, geriatric

The book advises feeding an adult cat on a regular routine: "Feed an adult cat once or twice a day". It says good feeding supports health: "If you give your cat a well-balanced diet, it will radiate good health". Amounts depend on the cat and the food label.

```claim
id: cat-feed-adult
task: Feed meals
cadence: daily
cadence_text: once or twice a day
cadence_quote: Feed an adult cat once or twice a day
importance: essential
importance_quote: If you give your cat a well-balanced diet, it will radiate good health
medication: no
```

## Feeding kittens

Stages: kitten

Young kittens eat small meals often, then fewer as they grow: "kittens should be fed little and often", with "Four or more meals a day for the kittens is normal", reducing to about three meals by three to four months and two at six months.

```claim
id: cat-feed-kitten
task: Feed kitten meals
cadence: daily
cadence_text: four or more small meals a day, falling to three by 3-4 months and two by 6 months
cadence_quote: Four or more meals a day for the kittens is normal
importance: essential
importance_quote: kittens should be fed little and often
medication: no
```

## Teeth cleaning

Stages: all

Cleaning a cat's teeth helps control tartar: "If tartar can be kept under control, the risk is reduced". The book gives owner brushing as an alternative to a veterinary scale and polish: "once or twice a week".

```claim

importance: recommended

medication: no
```
