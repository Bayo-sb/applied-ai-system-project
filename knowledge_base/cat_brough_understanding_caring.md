# Cat care: "Understanding, Caring for & Training Your Cat" (tasks only)

General guidance, not veterinary advice.

Species: cat
Stages: all

## Source

- Title: "Understanding, Caring for & Training Your Cat"
- Author: Paul Brough, eBookwholesaler ebook, (c) 2007, 102 pages
- Local copy: `assets/Understanding-Caring-for-and-Training-Your-Cat.pdf` (not committed to the repository)
- Written by the author as "a broad overview by a lay person". Authority is low, so only routine owner tasks that do not conflict with the other cat sources are used. No health or medical claims are used. Quotes are short exact phrases. The rest is written in my own words.

## Weekly litter tray wash

Stages: all

The book calls the litter box necessary ("A litter box is essential for a cat at home") and says to change the litter daily. Once a week the tray should be washed out: "Once a week, empty litter completely and wash the tray thoroughly with detergent and hot water".

```claim

task: Empty and wash the litter tray
cadence: weekly
cadence_text: once a week
cadence_quote: Once a week, empty litter completely
importance: recommended
importance_quote: A litter box is essential for a cat at home
medication: no
```

## Washing food and water bowls

Stages: all

## Play sessions

Stages: all

Cats do not play for long. The book suggests "Brief spells of play normally early in the morning and evening", at regular times. The book adds: "It also maintains the physical health of your cat".

```claim
id: cat-play-daily
task: Play with the cat
cadence: daily
cadence_text: briefly, morning and evening
cadence_quote: Brief spells of play normally early in the morning and evening
importance: recommended
importance_quote: It also maintains the physical health of your cat
medication: no
```

## Kitten training sessions

Stages: kitten, junior

For a young cat the book advises daily short training: "Repeat training sessions every day. Keep it short (for fifteen minutes only)". Using the same commands in the same place "helps cats to understand commands more easily and quickly".

```claim
medication: no

```
